# Spain

Reference for a small business established in or selling into mainland Spain and the Balearic
Islands. Figures marked **(checked 16/10/2026)** were re-verified that day; everything else was
researched on 22/06/2026. The Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla are outside the EU VAT area and
have their own indirect taxes (IGIC, IPSI): never apply the mainland VAT rates there. Reference,
not legal or tax advice.

## VAT (IVA)

- **Rates:** 21% standard; 10% reduced (passenger transport, hotels, restaurants, most food
  outside the 4% basket, new homes); 4% super-reduced (basic foods, olive oil, books and
  newspapers, medicines). The temporary food and energy cuts of 2022 to 2024 ended on
  01/01/2025.
- **Registration:** no domestic threshold for residents or non-residents. Small businesses use
  special schemes, not a turnover exemption. Non-EU businesses generally need a fiscal
  representative.
- **Returns:** Modelo 303 quarterly by default (Q1 to Q3 by the 20th of the following month, Q4
  by 30 January), monthly for large businesses; Modelo 390 annual summary by 30 January; Modelo
  349 for intra-EU sales.
- **SII (real-time ledger reporting)** is mandatory above EUR 6,010,121.04 turnover, for VAT
  groups and monthly-refund registrants: invoice records within 4 working days.
- **Equivalence surcharge:** sole-trader retailers selling goods unaltered pay a surcharge to
  their suppliers instead of filing VAT returns.

## Invoicing, VeriFactu and B2B e-invoicing

- **Full invoice (RD 1619/2012):** sequential number in a series, date, supply date if different,
  issuer and recipient names, tax IDs (NIF) and addresses, description, base, rate and VAT per
  rate, total; any withholding; "inversión del sujeto pasivo" for reverse charge.
- **Simplified invoices** up to EUR 400 (EUR 3,000 in some sectors); not deductible by the buyer
  unless the buyer's NIF and address and the VAT are added.
- **Keep records 6 years** (commercial code), longer than the 4-year tax limitation period.
- **VeriFactu** sets rules for billing software: each invoice record hashed and chained to the
  previous one, a QR code on every invoice, no alteration, and optional real-time sending to the
  tax agency. Businesses already on SII are outside it. In law today (RD-law 15/2025) it applies
  from **01/01/2027** for corporate-tax payers and **01/07/2027** for everyone else. In October
  2026 a further deferral to **October 2028** was announced but had not been published in the BOE
  when checked on 16/10/2026: tell the reader both and to check the BOE.
- **B2B e-invoicing (Ley 18/2022, RD 238/2026, Order HAC/1028/2026):** structured EN 16931
  e-invoices for all domestic B2B, including the self-employed, plus reporting of invoice
  acceptance and payment dates. The Order was published in the BOE on 05/10/2026 and took effect
  on 06/10/2026. Obligations start on **06/10/2027** for businesses with turnover over EUR 8m and
  on **06/10/2028** for all others; status reporting for individuals and partnerships under the
  income-attribution regime from **06/10/2029**. Exchange through the tax agency's free public
  solution (UBL) or an authorised private platform.
- **VeriFactu and the B2B mandate are separate obligations.** One governs how software records
  invoices; the other how businesses exchange them. Most businesses will need both.
- **Public sector (B2G):** electronic invoices through FACe in Facturae format, mandatory since
  2015 above EUR 5,000.

## Company forms and formation

- **Sociedad Limitada (SL):** the default. Minimum capital EUR 1 since Ley 18/2022 (was EUR
  3,000). Below EUR 3,000, 20% of profit goes to the legal reserve until reserve plus capital
  reaches EUR 3,000, and shareholders are jointly liable for the shortfall up to EUR 3,000 in an
  insolvent liquidation, so many founders still put in EUR 3,000.
- **Sociedad Anónima (SA):** EUR 60,000, a quarter paid at formation.
- The SLNE was abolished by Ley 18/2022.
- **Steps:** name certificate from the central commercial register, NIE for each foreign founder
  or director, capital deposit, notarial deed, registration in the provincial commercial register,
  then tax ID and VAT registration (Modelo 036). Through the online CIRCE route with standard
  articles, an SL can be running in 2 to 3 working days.
- **Beneficial owners** (over 25%) are declared to the central register within a month.

| Taxpayer | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General | 25% | 25% | 25% | 25% |
| Micro (under EUR 1m), first EUR 50,000 | 21% | 19% | 17% | 17% |
| Micro, remainder | 22% | 21% | 20% | 20% |
| Small (under EUR 10m) | 24% | 23% | 22% | 21% |
| New companies, first two profitable years | 15% | 15% | 15% | 15% |

- **Modelo 200:** within 25 days after six months from the year end (1 to 25 July for a
  calendar year). **Modelo 202** instalments in April, October and December.
- **Accounts:** prepared within 3 months, approved within 6, deposited at the commercial
  register within a month of approval. Books legalised within 4 months.
- **Withholding:** 19% on dividends, interest and most royalties to residents.

## Self-employed (autónomo)

Registered with the tax agency (Modelo 036 or 037) and the self-employed social security scheme
(RETA). Since 2023 contributions follow real net income across 15 bands; for 2026 the monthly
amounts were frozen at 2025 levels apart from the intergenerational levy (about EUR 206 to EUR
623 a month). New autónomos pay a flat EUR 80 a month for the first 12 months. Professional
invoices to Spanish businesses usually carry 15% income tax withholding (7% in the first years).

## Employment

- **SMI:** EUR 1,221/month in 14 payments (EUR 17,094/year; EUR 40.70/day) from 01/01/2026.
  Salaries are normally expressed in 14 payments; the two extra payments may be spread over 12.
- **Contracts:** indefinite is the default; temporary contracts only for defined causes.
  Probation up to 6 months for qualified technicians, 2 months for others.
- **Working time:** 40 hours a week averaged over the year; 80 hours a year of overtime. The
  proposed 37.5-hour week was rejected by Congress on 10/09/2025.
- **Leave:** 30 calendar days a year; birth leave 19 weeks per parent.
- **Social security 2026:** common contingencies 28.30% (23.60% employer, 4.70% employee) plus
  unemployment, training and wage-guarantee contributions (employer total roughly 30% to 31%);
  maximum base EUR 5,101.20 a month, with a solidarity levy above it.
- **Dismissal:** objective dismissal 20 days' pay per year (max 12 months); unfair dismissal 33
  days per year (max 24 months). A prior hearing is required before a disciplinary dismissal.
- **Collective agreements (convenios)** cover most employees and often override the statutory
  floor.

## Data protection

GDPR plus the LOPDGDD (Ley Orgánica 3/2018), enforced by the AEPD, one of the most active EU
regulators. Specifics: digital age of consent 14; a DPO appointment must be notified to the
AEPD within 10 days; the cookie guide requires "reject" on the same layer and as easy as
"accept"; an internal whistleblowing channel is mandatory from 50 employees (Ley 2/2023);
commercial email needs prior consent or an existing customer relationship (LSSI art. 21).
