# Denmark

Reference for a small business established in or selling into Denmark. Currency is the Danish
krone (DKK); Denmark is in the EU but not the euro. Figures marked **(checked 16/10/2026)** were
re-verified that day; everything else was researched on 22/06/2026. The Faroe Islands and
Greenland are outside the EU VAT area. Reference, not legal or tax advice.

## VAT (moms)

- **Rate:** 25%, with no reduced rate. A narrow 0% set (for example newspapers, intra-EU supplies
  and exports); financial services, insurance, health and education are exempt.
- **Registration:** a Danish business registers once taxable turnover exceeds DKK 50,000 in a
  calendar year (the threshold has been DKK 50,000 since 2002; from 01/01/2025 it is measured per
  calendar year, not over any rolling 12 months). A non-established business has no threshold;
  a non-EU business generally needs a fiscal representative.
- **Return frequency:** half-yearly below DKK 5m turnover, quarterly from DKK 5m to 50m, monthly
  above. New registrations usually file quarterly for about the first 18 months. Filed through
  TastSelv Erhverv (E-tax for businesses), paid through the tax account.
- **EU SME scheme (from 2025):** EU businesses with EU-wide turnover up to EUR 100,000 may use
  another member state's small-business exemption under that state's rules.

## Bookkeeping and e-invoicing

- **Bookkeeping Act (Bogføringsloven, 2022):** businesses in scope keep records in a registered
  digital bookkeeping system that can send and receive structured e-invoices, store records for
  5 years, and export a standard audit file. Companies filing annual reports came in from 2024
  and 2025; sole proprietors and associations with turnover above DKK 300,000 two years running
  from financial years starting on or after 01/01/2026.
- **Formats:** OIOUBL and Peppol BIS 3.0 over the NemHandel network. Invoicing the public sector
  electronically has been mandatory for years. The business authority has signalled a move to a
  Peppol BIS 4-based format, with no fixed date.

## Company forms and filings

| Form | Danish | Minimum capital | Liability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Private limited company | ApS | DKK 20,000 | limited |
| Public limited company | A/S | DKK 400,000 | limited |
| Sole proprietorship | enkeltmandsvirksomhed | none | unlimited |
| General partnership | I/S | none | unlimited, joint |

Capital must be fully paid before the CVR number is issued. Registration is online through
virk.dk; the CVR register is public and free to search. The low-capital IVS form closed to new
companies in 2019.

**Annual report:** ApS and A/S file digitally with the Danish Business Authority within **6
months** of the year end (class B and C; the deadline moved permanently from 5 to 6 months for
years ending on or after 31/12/2021), or 4 months for class D. Sole proprietorships do not file
one. Late filing brings fines per management member and, if it persists, compulsory dissolution.

## Corporate tax

- **Rate:** 22% for resident companies (higher rates apply to oil and gas; shipping can elect
  tonnage tax).
- **Return:** generally due 6 months after the income year end (30 June for a calendar year).
- **Tax on account:** two instalments, 20 March and 20 November, with optional top-up payments.
- **Planned change:** the government platform of June 2026 proposes cutting the rate by 3 points
  over three years, from 22% to 19%, excluding the financial sector. A bill was listed for
  February in the 2026-27 programme; nothing was enacted when checked on 16/10/2026 and the timing
  of the first step was not set. Quote 22% as the law.

## Payroll and employment

- **No statutory minimum wage.** Pay floors come from collective agreements, which cover most of
  the labour market. Check whether one applies.
- **Payroll:** register as an employer on virk.dk; report through the income register
  (eIndkomst). Deductions: labour-market contribution 8% of gross (from the year the employee
  turns 18), then income tax per the employee's tax card; ATP pension a fixed monthly amount
  (about DKK 297 for full time, two thirds employer). Pay into the employee's NemKonto.
- **Salaried Employees Act (Funktionærloven):** employer notice from 1 month (under 6 months'
  service) rising to 6 months (over 9 years), to the end of a month; the employee gives 1 month.
- **Holiday Act:** 25 days a year accrued at 2.08 a month; 3 consecutive weeks may be taken in
  the main holiday period (1 May to 30 September).
- **Working time:** no general statutory weekly cap; the EU 48-hour average applies.

## Data protection

GDPR plus the Data Protection Act (Act No. 502 of 23/05/2018), supervised by Datatilsynet.
Specifics: the civil registration (CPR) number has extra protection and may be processed only
where law requires it, with consent, or for unambiguous identification; digital age of consent
13. **Datatilsynet cannot fine directly**: it reports cases to the police and fines are imposed by
the courts, within the GDPR ceilings.
