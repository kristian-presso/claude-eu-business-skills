## VAT (Umsatzsteuer)

- **Rates:** 19% standard; 7% reduced (most food, books, newspapers, public transport, some
  cultural items). Financial services and certain medical and education supplies are exempt.
- **Small-business scheme (Kleinunternehmerregelung, §19 UStG):** a Germany-established business
  may stay out of VAT if prior-year turnover was at most EUR 25,000 and current-year turnover
  stays at most EUR 100,000 (from 01/01/2025; previously EUR 22,000 and EUR 50,000). Crossing
  EUR 100,000 ends the scheme **immediately**, from that supply. Under the scheme you charge no
  VAT, reclaim none, and state on invoices that VAT is not charged under §19 UStG.
- **Non-established businesses:** no threshold; register before the first taxable supply made in
  Germany, unless OSS or the reverse charge covers it ([eu-wide.md](eu-wide.md)).
- **Advance returns (Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung)** via ELSTER: monthly if prior-year VAT was over
  EUR 9,000, quarterly between EUR 2,000 and EUR 9,000, annual return only below EUR 2,000
  (thresholds since 01/01/2025). New businesses usually file monthly at first. Due the 10th of
  the month after the period; a permanent one-month extension (Dauerfristverlängerung) is
  available, for monthly filers against a special prepayment of 1/11 of the prior year's VAT.
  An annual return is filed as well.

## Invoicing and e-invoicing

- **Full invoice (§14 UStG):** supplier and customer name and address; supplier's tax number or
  VAT ID; invoice date; a unique sequential number; quantity and description; date of supply;
  net amount by rate; rate; VAT amount, or a note on reverse charge or exemption. A buyer cannot
  reclaim input VAT on an invoice missing mandatory fields.
- **Small invoices** (gross at most EUR 250, §33 UStDV) need only supplier name and address,
  date, description, gross amount and rate. Not for reverse-charge or cross-border invoices.
- **E-invoicing (Wachstumschancengesetz):** a structured EN 16931 invoice (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD
  from 2.0.1, or another EN 16931 format) for domestic B2B supplies. A PDF is not an e-invoice.
  - From **01/01/2025** every business must be able to **receive** one; an email inbox is enough.
  - From **01/01/2027** businesses with prior-year turnover over **EUR 800,000** must **issue**
    them; others may still send paper or PDF with the recipient's consent until end of 2027.
  - From **01/01/2028** all businesses must issue them.
  - B2C and small invoices up to EUR 250 are outside the issuing obligation.

## Company forms and formation

| | GmbH | UG (haftungsbeschränkt) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum share capital | EUR 25,000 | EUR 1 |
| Paid in before registration | at least half (EUR 12,500) | all of it, in cash |
| Profit retention | none | 25% of annual profit into reserves until capital reaches EUR 25,000 |
| Liability | limited | limited |

Steps: articles of association, notarisation (in person or by approved video identification),
capital deposit, registration in the commercial register (Handelsregister) via the notary, then
trade-office registration (Gewerbeanmeldung) and tax registration with the Finanzamt. The company
exists only once registered. Typical cost EUR 1,000 to 2,500 including notary; registration
commonly takes 2 to 4 weeks (practitioner figures; verify locally).

## Annual accounts

Accounts under the Commercial Code (HGB), scaled by size class, published through the company
register (Unternehmensregister, formerly the Bundesanzeiger route) within 12 months of the year
end. Late filing starts fine proceedings, from EUR 2,500. Size-class thresholds are uplifted from
time to time: confirm the current ones before classifying.

## Corporate taxes

- **Corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer):** 15%, plus the solidarity surcharge of 5.5% of that
  tax, so 15.825%.
- **Trade tax (Gewerbesteuer):** 3.5% times the municipal multiplier (Hebesatz); big cities are
  commonly 400% to 490%, so roughly 14% to 17%.
- **Combined:** typically about 30%, depending on the municipality.
- **Scheduled cut:** the law for an immediate tax investment programme (BGBl. I 2025 No. 161,
  promulgated 18/07/2025) cuts corporation tax by one point a year from assessment year 2028:
  14%, 13%, 12%, 11%, then 10% from 2032.

## Employment

- **Minimum wage:** EUR 13.90/hour from 01/01/2026, rising to EUR 14.60 from 01/01/2027.
- **Mini-jobs:** earnings ceiling EUR 603/month in 2026, EUR 633 in 2027 (it follows the minimum
  wage).
- **Notice:** 2 weeks during probation (up to 6 months); then at least 4 weeks to the 15th or
  month end, lengthening for the employer with service (up to 7 months). The employee's minimum
  stays 4 weeks. Collective agreements may differ.
- **Social security:** shared between employer and employee; ceilings and the statutory health
  opt-out threshold reset every January. Pull current rates from the Deutsche
  Rentenversicherung and the health fund before costing a hire.

## Data protection

GDPR plus the Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG) and state laws. A **data protection officer is
required where at least 20 people regularly process personal data by automated means** (§38
BDSG), far lower than the GDPR's own triggers, and regardless of size where a DPIA is required.
Private businesses answer to the data protection authority of their federal state (16 of them),
not a single national regulator. GDPR fine ceilings apply ([eu-wide.md](eu-wide.md)).
