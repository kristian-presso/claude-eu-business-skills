# Greece

**(checked 16/10/2026)** were re-verified that day; everything else was researched on
22/06/2026. Greek tax and labour rules change often and e-invoicing dates have moved more than
once. Reference, not legal or tax advice.

## VAT (ΦΠΑ)

- **Rates:** 24% standard; 13% (fresh food, non-alcoholic drinks, restaurants, hotels, passenger
  transport); 6% (medicines, books, electricity and gas, theatre and cinema tickets). Some Aegean
  islands have had reduced rates; the list has been narrowed and suspended repeatedly, so verify
  for any specific island.
- **Registration:** no general turnover threshold; a business registers when it starts. Annual
  turnover up to EUR 10,000 can opt into the small-business exemption (no periodic returns, but
  still registered, invoicing and keeping books).
- VAT data flows through myDATA, so VAT returns are expected to reconcile to the electronic
  books.

## myDATA and B2B e-invoicing

**myDATA** is the tax authority's (AADE) real-time electronic-books platform, mandatory since
2021: income and expense documents and their classifications are transmitted and each gets a
unique identifier (MARK).

**B2B e-invoicing** (EU derogation, February 2023), domestic B2B and sales to non-EU businesses;
intra-EU B2B stays optional:

- **First group** (2023 revenue over EUR 1m): mandatory from **02/03/2026**, transition to
  **03/05/2026** (Decision A.1044/2026).
- **Everyone else:** mandatory from **02/11/2026**. Joint Decision A.1197/2026 (ΦΕΚ Β
  5905/30-9-2026) moved this from 01/10/2026. Businesses that filed their start declaration with
  a start date no later than 02/11/2026 may keep using older issuing routes (ERP issuance, the
  manual form) during a transition to **31/01/2027**; from 01/02/2027 invoices go through a
  licensed provider or AADE's own free apps (timologio, myDATAapp).
- **Penalties:** not issuing or transmitting is treated as non-issuance: 50% of the VAT on a
  VATable transaction; fixed fines (roughly EUR 500 to EUR 1,000) otherwise. Treat exact figures
  as time-sensitive.

## Company forms and formation

- **IKE (private capital company, Law 4072/2012):** the default for small companies; EUR 1
  minimum capital, limited liability, single member allowed, double-entry books.
- **AE (société anonyme):** EUR 25,000 minimum capital, heavier governance.
- **EPE:** the older limited company form, largely replaced by the IKE.
- **OE, EE:** partnerships; **sole trader:** no separate legal personality.
- **Steps:** formation through the GEMI one-stop shop: name check, standard articles for a simple
  IKE, filing, GEMI number and tax number (ΑΦΜ), e-EFKA registration, bank account. A clean
  filing is commonly approved in a few working days; fees are modest. Verify current GEMI fees.

## Corporate and withholding tax

- **Corporate income tax:** 22% for IKE, AE, EPE and partnerships taxed as entities (29% for
  credit institutions in a special regime).
- **Dividend withholding:** 5%; 0% under the EU parent-subsidiary directive (at least 5% held for
  24 months).
- **Personal income tax** runs from 9% to 44%; check the current bands for the tax year.

## Employment and payroll

- **Minimum wage:** EUR 920/month gross for employees and EUR 41.09/day for manual workers from
  01/04/2026 (up from EUR 880 and EUR 39.30). It is set each April by ministerial decision.
- **14 salaries a year:** 12 monthly plus a Christmas bonus (one month), an Easter bonus (half)
  and a holiday allowance (half).
- **Social security (e-EFKA):** about 35% of gross in total (employee about 13.4%, employer about
  21.8%, rates from 01/01/2025) up to the monthly cap of EUR 7,761.94 for 2026. Paid by the end
  of the following month.
- **Leave:** 20 working days a year on a 5-day week (24 on a 6-day week).
- **Working time:** 40-hour week; premiums for overtime, Sundays, holidays and nights. Contracts
  and working-time changes are filed in the ERGANI system; the Digital Work Card records hours
  in covered sectors.
- **Notice and severance** scale with service; giving full notice roughly halves the severance.

## Data protection

GDPR plus Law 4624/2019, supervised by the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr). Breach
notification within 72 hours; Law 4624/2019 sets national rules on employment data and the age
of consent. GDPR fine ceilings apply to private bodies.

## Other duties

Law 5135/2024 replaced stamp duty (repealed from 01/12/2024) with a digital transaction duty at
0.30%, 1.20%, 2.40% or 3.60% depending on the transaction (mainly loans and credit). Real estate
transfer tax is 3.09%; capital increases carry 0.2% capital duty. Check AADE circulars for a
specific transaction.
